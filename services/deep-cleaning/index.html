FAQ

Deep Cleaning questions

How often should I book a deep clean?

Most homes benefit from a deep clean two to four times a year, or as the first visit before recurring service begins.

Is the inside of the oven and fridge included?

Inside the oven and refrigerator are available as add-on services, so you only pay for what you need. Just mention them when you request a quote.

How long does a deep clean take?

A deep clean usually takes about twice as long as a standard clean. We will confirm timing based on your home's size and condition.
