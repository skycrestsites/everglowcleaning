FAQ

Questions, answered

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Yuba City and serve Marysville, Live Oak, Sutter, Plumas Lake, Olivehurst, Linda, Wheatland, Gridley and Biggs, along with the surrounding Yuba-Sutter area. Not sure if you are in range? Send us your address and we will confirm.

How much does a cleaning cost?

Every home is different, so pricing depends on the size of your space, its condition and how often you would like service. Request a free quote and we will send clear, upfront pricing tailored to your space before we begin.

Are your cleaners background checked?

Yes. Every member of our team is background checked before they ever step into a client's home or business.

Do I need to be home during my cleaning?

No. Many clients share entry instructions and come home to a finished clean. If you would rather be there, that is completely fine too.

Do you offer recurring cleaning plans?

Yes. Choose weekly, bi-weekly or monthly visits. Recurring clients get priority scheduling and a checklist tailored to their home.

Do you clean businesses as well as homes?

Yes. While most of our work is residential, we also provide customized commercial cleaning for offices, retail spaces and other commercial properties.
